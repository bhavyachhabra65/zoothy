Zoothy started with a simple thought: running a business is already hard enough. The software you use shouldn't make it harder.

We are building Zoothy for the person who has started a business, not for someone who has spent years studying accounts. You might be working alone, with one or two people, or just getting your startup off the ground. You should still be able to manage your everyday business work yourself.

So we are taking the things businesses need and making them straightforward. No accounting tuition. No complicated language. No need to build a whole accounts department just to use your own business software.

We have already started with two things businesses use all the time: printing cheques and making invoices. They are available today, free to use, and you don't even need an account to get started.

And this is only the beginning. We are building Zoothy into a wider workspace for money, business, documents and reports — with the same idea behind every module: if you can understand your business, you should be able to understand Zoothy.

Most importantly, we are building all of it to be free. The tools available today are free, and the modules we add tomorrow are being built with the same promise.
